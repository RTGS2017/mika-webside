Near Duplicates Versus Real Coverage

Real coverage answers a distinct question, while near-duplicate URLs mostly repeat the same page.

Educational material only. Mika does not guarantee search rankings or AI answer citations.

Definition

A near-duplicate is a template that changes little more than a label while the topic, intent, and evidence stay the same. That pattern is not topic coverage, and adding more of those URLs is not SEO growth.

Short answer

SEO Growth looks for coverage, intent depth, and markets that are still unoccupied, which is separate from a health reading. Question coverage is marked answered, partial, or uncovered, and a pile of similar URLs does not fill an uncovered question.

What to check

  • Near-duplicate templates are grouped
  • Distinct topics are listed apart
  • Uncovered questions stay visible
  • Page count is not treated as growth

What this does not claim

Removing near-duplicates does not guarantee that an unanswered query will be covered or cited. Mika Visibility keeps health separate from growth, and it does not guarantee that fewer URLs will change visibility.

A practical example

Two product URLs that swap a place name but keep the same specifications and the same answer are near-duplicates, not two topics. A page that answers a different application, with its own constraints and evidence, is the kind of coverage SEO Growth is looking for.
