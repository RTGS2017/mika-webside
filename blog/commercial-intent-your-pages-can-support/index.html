Commercial Intent Your Pages Can Support

Informational, commercial, and transactional intents are different, and only honest support belongs in the opportunity list.

Educational material only. Mika does not guarantee search rankings or AI answer citations.

Definition

Informational intent asks to understand a topic, commercial intent compares or selects a product, and transactional intent is ready to act. A commercial opportunity here is a query the product page can honestly support with specifications, use cases, and proof already on the site.

Short answer

Mark commercial question coverage as covered, partial, or missing, and describe only the opportunities the page can support. Do not invent demand figures, and do not treat an informational explainer as if it were a transactional offer.

What to check

  • Intent is informational, commercial, or transactional
  • Specifications on the page support it
  • Use cases on the page match the query
  • Unsupported queries stay marked missing

What this does not claim

Labeling a query as commercial does not guarantee demand, traffic, or citation. Mika Visibility will not invent commercial facts, and it does not guarantee that a supported query will be recommended.

A practical example

If a product page already publishes specifications and a use case for a placement method, a commercial query about that method can be described as supportable. A query about a market the site never documents stays missing, because there is no honest proof to attach.
