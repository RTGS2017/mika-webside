From the First Scan Through the Next Re-audit

Scan, diagnose, blueprint, optimize, and re-audit keep each pass tied to reasons and to the next actions.

Educational material only. Mika does not guarantee search rankings or AI answer citations.

Definition

The loop is SCAN, then DIAGNOSE, then BLUEPRINT, then OPTIMIZE, then RE-AUDIT. Scanning collects the live site, diagnosis explains what failed, the blueprint chooses next actions, and optimize is the change that actually ships. Re-audit checks what shipped, so the loop closes on observed pages rather than on a mystery KPI.

Short answer

A useful report gives reasons, sample URLs, opportunities, and next actions. Re-audit opens the pages that were supposed to change and records what is live, which is how the next scan starts from evidence.

What to check

  • Start from a scan of the site
  • Diagnose before changing pages
  • Put next actions in a blueprint
  • Re-audit the pages that shipped

What this does not claim

Finishing a scan or a re-audit does not guarantee rankings, citations, or a place in an answer product. The loop reports what was observed and what to do next, and it does not sell a hidden number that predicts the future.

A practical example

A blueprint might call for canonical repairs on a set of sample URLs and a clearer answer on one product page. The re-audit then requests those same URLs, checks whether the repairs are live, and writes down what is still open.
