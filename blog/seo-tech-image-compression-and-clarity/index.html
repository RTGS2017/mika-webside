Compression Trades File Weight Against Visible Damage

Image compression reduces bytes and can leave visible artifacts when it is pushed too far.

Short answer

Image compression reduces bytes and can leave visible artifacts when it is pushed too far.

A product edge that turns into blocks is not a faster hero in any useful sense. The buyer cannot see the fitting, and the largest paint may be slightly earlier for a picture that no longer does its job. SEO Health can note both weight and damage. It does not pick a numeric quality slider for every future upload.

Definition

The artifact image on this page is a teaching close-up, not a before-and-after of a customer. Formats such as modern still-image encodings often hold edges better than older ones at the same byte size, but the template still needs a human look at the product boundary. A CDN parameter that requests a very small quality number should be reviewed on the hero, not only on a thumbnail.

What belongs in the page basics

Check the hero at the quality the template actually requests, and reject settings that break product edges.

Thumbnails can tolerate harder compression than the image that fills the first screen. One global quality number applied to both is how the hero gets damaged or the thumbnail stays heavy. The audit sample is a hero URL and a thumbnail URL from the same template. No universal percentage belongs in the note.

What to check

  • Hero quality reviewed visually, not only by file size
  • A separate, harder setting allowed for thumbnails
  • No single quality number applied to every slot
  • One hero URL and one thumbnail URL
  • No invented percentage quoted as a standard

A practical example

A pipeline exports every upload at a very low quality because last quarter’s hero was too heavy. Connector threads disappear into blocks. The repair is a higher quality on the hero ladder and a smaller pixel size, not a return to the camera original. The pipeline change is template-level, so one sample reveals it.

What this does not claim

A clearer compressed image does not guarantee rankings or citations.
