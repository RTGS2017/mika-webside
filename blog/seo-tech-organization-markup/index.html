Organization Markup States Who You Are, in Fields the Page Also Shows

Organization markup names the company, URL, and logo the site already shows.

Short answer

Organization markup names the company, URL, and logo the site already shows.

The knowledge-panel picture is an example of a presentation, not a panel this markup will produce. SEO Health checks that the legal name, the canonical homepage URL, and the logo match the about page. Extra social profiles should be real profiles, not empty fields added because a generator listed them.

Definition

One organization entity should describe one company. Marking every product page as a different organization because the template pastes the block everywhere is noisy but less harmful than pasting a different name on each page. Keep the name stable. GEO’s entity work still needs the prose to say who you are. The markup only repeats it.

What the markup can and cannot do

Use one stable organization name, homepage URL, and logo that match the about page.

Do not add awards, ratings, or customer lists in organization markup that the site does not show. Those fields become false claims in a machine-readable place. Mika Visibility does not publish customer logos or awards as proof, and this article will not instruct a site to invent them in JSON-LD.

What to check

  • One name matching the about page
  • Canonical homepage URL and one logo
  • No awards or customers added only in markup
  • The same entity on templates that repeat the block
  • A sample about URL and a sample product URL compared

A practical example

The about page says “Northline Components” and the markup says “Northline” on some templates and “Northline Inc.” on others, with three logo files. The repair picks the about-page name, one logo URL, and the https homepage. The panel illustration is not the test. The match is.

What this does not claim

Organization markup does not guarantee a knowledge panel, rankings, or citations.

Where Mika Visibility files this

Mika Visibility treats structured data as a technical SEO Health topic: the markup should match visible facts and parse cleanly. It does not sell rich-result placement. GEO still cares that entities and evidence are understandable in the prose. Markup that repeats a slogan does not create evidence.

The panel image is an example of a presentation, not a deliverable of the markup.
