Direct answer

A commercial investigation page should help a buyer judge fit before contact: the product, the use, and the constraint. SEO Growth is the name for that still-open job. It is not a ranking target and it is not GEO.

Why it matters

Commercial intent is not the same as a slogan about quality. The page compares constraints the product can honestly support: range, exclusion, and the document needed before a conversation. If the only next step is a form, and the form is where the limit appears, the URL the buyer opened is still partial. Do not invent competitor rankings to make the comparison look decided.

What to check

Label the intent before choosing a URL. Keep informational definitions, commercial fit, and the transactional next step on the URLs that own those jobs. For this commercial job, write the constraint next to the claim and link the specification.

  • Name the product and the constraint on one URL.
  • Say what does not fit.
  • Link the document a buyer needs before contact.
  • Do not publish invented competitor ranks.

A practical example

A buyer asks which liner suits a named chemical. The category page offers a demo and never names an exclusion. The repair adds the compatible family and the exclusion on that category URL, or on the one product URL that owns the answer, and links the datasheet. No win rate is claimed.

Content depth

Depth here is the fit and the non-fit. A long page of benefits without an exclusion is thin for a commercial question. A short page that states both can be enough.

How an unanswered topic gets found

Link from the page where the comparison starts, using the buyer’s words. One canonical URL should own the answer. A second thin comparison splits the path. Re-audit that the constraint is in the HTML. That is not a forecast.

Commercial coverage is an unanswered fit question, not a thinner homepage. Mika Visibility files this on the SEO Growth track: coverage, intent depth, and markets that are still open. It is not an SEO Health score and it is not GEO. Page count is not growth. There is no blended overall score.
