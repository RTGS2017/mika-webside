Four Independent Visibility Tracks Without a Blended Score

Health and growth answer different questions, and mixing them into one grade makes the next action harder to see.

Definition

SEO Health asks whether the live site is technically ready to be found and understood, covering technical setup, structure, and content. GEO Health asks whether people can find a clear supportable answer from the site itself, which is a question about understanding, evidence, and answers. SEO Growth asks which coverage, intent depth, and markets are still unoccupied, while GEO Growth asks where answer coverage, citation readiness, and expansion can still improve. Because a high health score never becomes an automatic growth score, Mika Visibility keeps the four readings independent and does not publish a blended Overall Score.

Short answer

The model is a two-by-two of SEO or GEO crossed with Health or Growth, and each cell answers a different question. A site can be healthy and still hard to discover, so a health result is never converted into a growth result.

What to check

  • Record SEO Health on its own
  • Record GEO Health on its own
  • Keep growth apart from health
  • Do not blend an overall score

What this does not claim

Keeping the four tracks independent does not guarantee a search position or a place on an answer surface. Mika Visibility explains structured SEO and GEO audits, and it does not sell rankings or fabricated commercial facts.

A practical example

A crawl can show clean status codes while a second market still has no page that honestly supports commercial intent. SEO Health and SEO Growth then remain side by side, and those two readings are not averaged into one grade.
