Source-Backed Page Pattern

English and Chinese pages should share the same facts in the same source-backed order.

Educational material only. Mika does not guarantee search rankings or AI answer citations.

On this page

  1. Definition
  2. Short answer
  3. What to check
  4. What this does not claim
  5. A practical example
  6. FAQ

Definition

The source-backed order places a definition first, then a short answer, then an explanation or example, and then the sources. English and Chinese share those facts so a reader in either language sees the same claims with the same support.

Short answer

Follow that order and keep the facts aligned between the root English page and the Chinese page under /zh/. Clear structure helps a page be understandable and referenceable, and structure alone is not a promise of being quoted.

What to check

  • Definition comes before the answer
  • A short answer follows the definition
  • An example or explanation is present
  • Sources carry the same shared facts

What this does not claim

Using this page order does not guarantee quotation, recommendation, or inclusion in ChatGPT, Gemini, or AI Overviews. GEO means Generative Engine Optimization, and a tidy structure does not guarantee citation.

A practical example

A page can define a product constraint, answer who it is for, show how a direct answer names the use case and the limits, and then point to the specification source. The Chinese page under /zh/ should carry those same facts rather than introducing a different claim.
