Direct answer

A page that answers a question is still hard to discover if no internal link points at it with words a buyer would recognize. SEO Growth includes that path. “Click here” is not the path.

Why it matters

Orphan pages have no internal links and a weak discovery path. Broken internal links waste the path and the reader’s trust. The anchor should name the product, the question, or the document, not a generic verb. Links should stay in the language the reader is using. A Chinese answer that is linked only from English is half-orphaned.

What to check

From the page where the question starts, add one link to the owner. Use the question or the product name as the anchor. Confirm the target returns 200 and is the canonical. Remove links that point at retired URLs.

  • Give every important answer at least one in-language internal link.
  • Use an anchor that names the destination.
  • Fix broken targets.
  • Do not orphan a translation.

A practical example

The rotor limit is written on a note that nothing links to. The product page says “learn more.” The repair changes the anchor to “rotor limit above the stated speed” and points at the note. Re-audit the link. No click-through rate is invented.

Content depth

The link does not replace the answer. It makes the answer reachable. A page with excellent anchors and no sentence is still uncovered.

How an unanswered topic gets found

After the link exists, the sitemap may list the URL. The sitemap does not replace the link. A re-audit checks that the anchor resolves and the answer is still on the target.
