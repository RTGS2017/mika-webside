Company Information Buyers Can Verify

Buyers should be able to check company claims against specifications, process notes, and other verifiable context on the site.

Educational material only. Mika does not guarantee search rankings or AI answer citations.

Definition

Company facts that buyers can verify answer who you are, what you sell, and where you operate, without hype. Evidence means specifications, process, and verifiable context placed next to the claim, not a slogan standing alone.

Short answer

Keep those facts inside the entity graph that runs from the company to products, applications, technical information, documentation, and evidence. Do not invent certifications or commercial claims the site cannot show.

What to check

  • Who you are is stated plainly
  • What you sell matches the products
  • Where you operate is stated as fact
  • Evidence sits next to each claim

What this does not claim

Publishing verifiable company facts does not guarantee trust in search results or citation by an answer engine. Mika Visibility does not invent certifications, and it does not guarantee that a complete profile will be recommended.

A practical example

A company block can name the products sold and the places served, then link each claim to documentation or a specification. A credential the site has not published is not evidence, so the page should leave it out rather than imply a certification that was never stated.
