Where GEO Growth Still Has Room

Definition

GEO Growth asks where answer coverage, citation readiness, and expansion can still improve after a clear answer already exists somewhere on the site. The list is made of query gaps and answer gaps, plus places where citation readiness is incomplete and where honest expansion is still possible. Readiness in this column is an unfinished-coverage note, not a promise that an answer surface will include the page.

Short answer

A site can already answer one question clearly and still leave a neighboring question partial or uncovered. Expansion means adding support for that gap, and citation readiness only describes whether the new answer is ready to be referenced.

What to check

  • List the query gaps still open
  • List the answer gaps still open
  • Judge citation readiness only
  • Plan expansion with evidence

What this does not claim

Stronger citation readiness does not guarantee inclusion, recommendation, or citation in ChatGPT, Gemini, AI Overviews, or any other AI product. A query gap is a planning note, not a forecast of visibility.

A practical example

A product page may already answer one application clearly while a related technical question still has no direct answer on the site. GEO Health can stay intact for the answered page while GEO Growth records the open gap and the evidence still needed to close it.
