A market page needs real depth, not a swapped label

Direct answer

A market page has real depth when that language or region URL answers a question a buyer in that market asks, while keeping the same product facts. Swapping a country name into one paragraph is not a market.

Why it matters

Markets are a SEO Growth tag, separate from SEO Health. A healthy English site can still have an uncovered Chinese question. The page you add must not be a thin mirror. It needs the shared facts and the local question. Do not invent local customers, licenses, or volumes to make the market look occupied.

What to check

List markets you can actually serve. For each, write the question that is different, or admit the question is the same and translate the full answer including limits. Publish under the real path, /zh/ for Chinese, with hreflang only after the page exists.

  • Serve only markets you can answer.
  • Keep product facts aligned.
  • Add the local question or do not publish the URL.
  • Avoid city-name copies.

A practical example

A template prints the same valve paragraph with a new city in the H1. The repair deletes the copies and, for the one market you serve, writes the voltage or the standard that market actually asks about, beside the unchanged pressure limit. No city ranking is claimed.

Content depth

Depth is the local question plus the shared specification. A map of the country is not depth. The answer has to be text.

How an unanswered topic gets found

Link the market URL from the language’s hub with an anchor that names the market question. Do not leave it orphaned in the sitemap. Re-audit both languages. Coverage is not a percentage from the homepage sample counters.

Market coverage is an answered local question, not a label swap. Mika Visibility files this on the SEO Growth track: coverage, intent depth, and markets that are still open. It is not an SEO Health score and it is not GEO. Page count is not growth. There is no blended overall score.

What this does not claim

A market page does not guarantee rankings in that country or AI citations. Homepage sample counters are not market results.
