Direct answer

A coverage map lists buyer questions, marks each answered, partial, or uncovered, and names the URL that owns it. SEO Growth uses the map to decide the next edit. Creating URLs first and mapping later produces duplicates.

Why it matters

The map is not a blended score and not the homepage’s sample counters. Those counters are illustrative interface. Your map is a list: question, intent, owner, state, and the missing sentence. Commercial questions can be cut separately as covered, partial, or missing without becoming a mystery KPI. The audit path is still audit, diagnosis, blueprint, then the page, then re-audit.

What to check

Start from questions, not from a keyword export alone. For each, open the live URL and see whether the answer is in HTML. Record partial when the topic is named and the constraint is missing. Only then brief a new URL.

  • List questions with a state and an owner.
  • Open the live HTML before adding a URL.
  • Separate commercial questions without inventing a KPI.
  • Re-audit after the blueprint ships.

A practical example

A spreadsheet of 40 proposed URLs collapses, on inspection, to 6 questions, 4 of which already have owners. The blueprint edits those 4 and briefs 2. The other 34 lines are not a roadmap. No opportunity score is published.

Content depth

The map is detailed enough when the next action is obvious: edit this sentence, or create one URL for one uncovered question. A heat map without owners is not.

How an unanswered topic gets found

After the blueprint ships, the new or edited URL needs an internal link and a sitemap entry. The map itself can stay unpublished. Re-audit moves the question’s state only when the live page changed.

The coverage map is the growth list. It is not an overall score. Mika Visibility files this on the SEO Growth track: coverage, intent depth, and markets that are still open. It is not an SEO Health score and it is not GEO. Page count is not growth. There is no blended overall score.

What this does not claim

A coverage map does not guarantee rankings or AI citations. Sample homepage counters are not your map.
