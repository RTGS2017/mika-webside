Consistent Facts Across Languages

Shared company and product facts stay aligned across languages, and a market-specific question still needs a page written for that market.

Educational material only. Mika does not guarantee search rankings or AI answer citations.

Definition

Consistent facts means six fields say the same thing in every language: company name, product name, specifications, applications, claims, and question coverage. English is published at the site root, Chinese is published under /zh/, and the site does not use an /en/ prefix.

Short answer

Align those six fields, then give market-specific questions their own pages instead of thin translated mirrors. Hreflang names en, zh-Hans, and x-default to English, and thin mirrors plus copy spam are the failure mode this check is meant to catch.

What to check

  • Company name matches in each language
  • Product name and specifications match
  • Applications and claims stay aligned
  • Question coverage is not a thin mirror

What this does not claim

Matching facts across languages does not guarantee inclusion, recommendation, or citation in any answer engine. The audit checks whether the six fields agree, and it does not guarantee a search outcome for a translated page.

A practical example

The site illustration names EN, 中文, Español, العربية, Русский, and Deutsch as language labels. If the Chinese page under /zh/ changes a specification that the English root page does not state, the fields no longer match, and a copied mirror would be the thin-page failure mode.
