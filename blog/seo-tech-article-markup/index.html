Article Markup Names a Piece You Already Published

Article markup repeats the headline, date, and publisher the page already shows.

Short answer

Article markup repeats the headline, date, and publisher the page already shows.

The rich-result picture is an illustration of a presentation some pages become eligible for. It is not a result owned by this site and not a layout you can demand. If the JSON-LD headline disagrees with the H1, the markup is wrong even when it parses. SEO Health fixes the disagreement.

Definition

Dates should be the publication or update the page is willing to show, not a build timestamp rewritten nightly. Author or publisher should be the organization a reader can find on the page, not a staff nickname that appears nowhere else. Mika Visibility’s own articles name Mika as the organization. That is a fact about this site, not a template to copy onto a customer.

What the markup can and cannot do

Make the markup headline, date, and publisher match the visible article, and do not expect the illustration as an outcome.

A blog index is not an article. Marking every card in a list as a full Article with a fake date is how contradictory entities appear. Mark the document that is actually the article, on its own URL. Validation can pass while the visible page still says something else, so read both.

What to check

  • Headline in markup equal to the visible H1
  • Dates that are real updates, not nightly build times
  • Publisher identifiable on the page
  • List cards not each marked as a full article with fake dates
  • One article URL where markup and H1 were compared

A practical example

A news-like update page shows the title “Seal kit change” and the markup headline is the site slogan with yesterday’s build time as datePublished. The repair copies the visible title and a real update date, or omits the date. The slogan stays in the layout if you want it, not in the article entity.

What this does not claim

Article markup does not guarantee rankings, rich results, or citations.

Where Mika Visibility files this

Mika Visibility treats structured data as a technical SEO Health topic: the markup should match visible facts and parse cleanly. It does not sell rich-result placement.
