Why a Healthy Site Can Still Be Hard to Find

Crawlable pages can still miss unanswered searches, buried questions, or thin answer surfaces.

Definition

SEO Health asks whether the site is technically ready to be found and understood, while SEO Growth asks about coverage, intent depth, and markets still unoccupied. A high health score is not a growth score, and GEO Health is likewise separate from GEO Growth.

Short answer

Four capabilities stay independent, with no blended overall score: SEO Health, GEO Health, SEO Growth, and GEO Growth. Crawlable pages can still miss unanswered searches, buried questions, or thin answer surfaces, so health and growth are reported apart.

What to check

  • SEO Health stays separate from growth
  • GEO Health is not a growth reading
  • Unanswered searches are listed as gaps
  • Thin answer surfaces are called out

What this does not claim

A healthy crawl does not guarantee that buyers will find the site or that answer engines will cite it. Mika Visibility does not sell fabricated commercial facts, and a health reading does not guarantee discovery.

A practical example

Pages can return sound status codes, sit on clean crawl paths, and still leave a buyer question uncovered or answered only in a buried paragraph. GEO Growth would mark the query gap, the answer gap, and citation readiness separately from the health reading.
