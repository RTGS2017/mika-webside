Citation Readiness Without Promising a Citation

A page can be supportable and referenceable and still never be included or recommended.

Educational material only. Mika does not guarantee search rankings or AI answer citations.

Definition

Citation readiness describes whether information is understandable, supportable, and referenceable. Understandable means a direct answer can be found, supportable means evidence is tied to the claim, and referenceable means the context is consistent enough that someone could point to the page. The measurement stops at that quality, and it does not turn readiness into inclusion.

Short answer

Source-backed pages carry a definition, a short answer, an example, and sources, and bilingual pages are expected to share the same facts. Those habits make a page easier to check, and they remain editorial practice rather than a method for being chosen by a model.

What to check

  • Make each answer understandable
  • Make each claim supportable
  • Make the context referenceable
  • Keep sources with the page

What this does not claim

Citation readiness does not guarantee inclusion, recommendation, or citation in any AI product, including ChatGPT, Gemini, and AI Overviews. Clearing warnings does not guarantee rankings or citations, and Mika Visibility does not sell answer-surface placement.

A practical example

A constraint on a product should sit next to specifications, process notes, or other verifiable context, with the same facts repeated in each language. A reader can then understand the answer and see what supports it, which still does not guarantee that an answer engine will quote the page.
