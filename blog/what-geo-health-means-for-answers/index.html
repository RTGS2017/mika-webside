What GEO Health Means for Answers

Generative Engine Optimization here is about whether answers are clear, not about geographic targeting.

Educational material only. Mika does not guarantee search rankings or AI answer citations.

  1. Definition
  2. Short answer
  3. What to check

Definition

GEO means Generative Engine Optimization, not geographic SEO, and GEO Health asks whether someone can find a clear supportable answer about the business from the site itself. The checks look for clear company and product entities, direct answers, evidence tied to claims, and consistent context across pages and languages. A practical path runs from the question to the topic, the intent, the entity, the evidence, and then a direct answer.

Short answer

Someone should be able to find the answer on the site, see what evidence supports it, and recognize the same context on related pages. GEO Health stops at that clarity and leaves query gaps and expansion to the growth reading.

What to check

  • Name the company and the products
  • Answer the question on the page
  • Tie each claim to evidence
  • Keep context aligned across pages

What this does not claim

A clear GEO Health reading does not guarantee inclusion, recommendation, or citation in any AI product, including ChatGPT, Gemini, or AI Overviews. The page can be understandable and still not be selected.

A practical example

Put verifiable context beside the claim, such as a specification, a process note, or a constraint a reader can check. The direct answer should name the use case and point to that evidence so the support can be read from the site itself.
