Does Mika guarantee AI citations?

Mika does not sell AI citations. Readiness to be attributed is a separate question from being included in an AI product.

Educational material only. Mika does not guarantee search rankings or AI answer citations.

Definition

Citation readiness means a careful reader could attribute a statement with less ambiguity. Mika does not sell guaranteed AI citations, and readiness is not inclusion in ChatGPT, Gemini, or AI Overviews.

Short answer

No. Citation readiness is not inclusion, recommendation, or citation in ChatGPT, Gemini, or AI Overviews. GEO does not promise that any AI product will quote the page.

What to check

  • Separate readiness from inclusion
  • Name ChatGPT, Gemini, Overviews
  • Require an attributable statement
  • Refuse any citation promise

What this does not claim

Citation readiness does not guarantee inclusion, recommendation, or citation in ChatGPT, Gemini, AI Overviews, or any other AI product. Mika does not guarantee that a careful, attributable page will be quoted.

A practical example

Suppose a page places a spec beside a product claim so a careful reader can attribute the statement. That citation readiness still does not mean ChatGPT, Gemini, or AI Overviews will include, recommend, or cite the page.
