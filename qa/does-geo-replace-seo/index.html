Does GEO replace SEO?

GEO does not replace SEO. Answer work still depends on pages that crawl and index work can reach.

Educational material only. Mika does not guarantee search rankings or AI answer citations.

Definition

GEO means Generative Engine Optimization, and it builds on pages that are already reachable. It does not replace the crawl and index work that SEO still has to do, and it is not geographic SEO.

Short answer

No. GEO builds on reachable pages and does not replace crawl and index work. Generative Engine Optimization still needs the SEO work that makes a page possible to reach and understand.

What to check

  • Confirm the page can be crawled
  • Confirm index work still matters
  • Treat GEO as built on reachable pages
  • Do not drop SEO for GEO

What this does not claim

Adding GEO on top of SEO does not guarantee inclusion in an AI answer. GEO does not guarantee that crawl and index work can be skipped or that a citation will follow.

A practical example

Suppose robots rules block a product URL, so a crawler cannot reach it. GEO cannot replace that SEO problem, because a generative-engine answer still depends on a page that can be reached, and GEO does not take over index work.
