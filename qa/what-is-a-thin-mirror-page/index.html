What is a thin mirror page?

A thin mirror page repeats another language without the depth that market actually needs.

Definition

A thin mirror page is a translated shell that repeats another language without real market coverage. It looks like a second language version, and it does not add market-specific depth.

Short answer

A thin mirror page repeats another language without market-specific depth. Translation alone does not create real market coverage for that language version.

What to check

  • Compare the translation with the source
  • Look for missing market-specific depth
  • Treat a shell as a thin mirror
  • Do not count it as real coverage

What this does not claim

Removing a thin mirror page does not guarantee better positions in that market. Mika does not guarantee that market-specific depth will produce a position or a citation.

A practical example

Suppose the Chinese URL under /zh/ translates the English root page line by line and adds no market-specific facts. That Chinese URL is a thin mirror page, because it repeats the other language without real market coverage.
