How should a source-backed page be ordered?

A source-backed page follows one order: definition, short answer, example, and sources.

Definition

Source-backed pages are ordered as definition, short answer, example, and sources. The sequence puts the meaning first, then the direct answer, then a concrete example, and then the sources that support the page.

Short answer

Order the page as definition, short answer, example, and sources. Readers meet the meaning, the answer, a concrete example, and the supporting sources in that sequence.

What to check

  • Definition comes first
  • Short answer comes next
  • Example follows the answer
  • Sources come last

What this does not claim

A practical example

A page can define an indexation mismatch, answer what it is in a short paragraph, give a sitemap-and-canonical example, and then list sources. That sequence is the source-backed order: definition, short answer, example, and sources.
