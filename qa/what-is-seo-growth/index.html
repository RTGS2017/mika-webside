Which coverage, intent depth, and markets are still unoccupied?

SEO Growth is the track for what is still open: coverage, intent depth, and markets.

Definition

SEO Growth asks which coverage, intent depth, and markets are still unoccupied. It is a separate track from SEO Health, so an open market is not folded into a technical readiness score.

Short answer

SEO Growth looks for coverage, intent depth, and markets that are still open. Those gaps stay on their own scale and are not blended with SEO Health.

What to check

  • List coverage that is still missing
  • Judge intent depth on real pages
  • Name markets that are still open
  • Keep growth separate from health

What this does not claim

Seeing an open market on the SEO Growth track does not guarantee that new pages will occupy it. Mika does not guarantee that deeper intent coverage will produce a position or a citation.

A practical example

Suppose the site explains one product line in depth and never answers a neighboring market's buyer questions. SEO Growth records that coverage and that market as still unoccupied, without blending the note into SEO Health.
