What are informational, commercial, and transactional intents?

Informational, commercial, and transactional are the three intents used when questions on the site are classified.

Definition

The intents used on the site are informational, commercial, and transactional. Commercial intent is a query a product page can honestly support with specifications, use cases, and proof, and informational and transactional are the other two intent labels kept separate from that commercial case.

Short answer

Informational, commercial, and transactional are the intents used on the site. A commercial query is one a product page can honestly support with specs, use cases, and proof, while the other two intents stay as separate labels.

What to check

  • Commercial intent is used
