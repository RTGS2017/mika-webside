What should you fix before a new market?

Before a new market, align company and product facts and avoid copy-spam mirrors that only repeat the same page.

Definition

Before a new market, align facts and avoid copy-spam mirrors. Pages for that market should share accurate company and product facts with the existing pages instead of repeating thin copies that add no distinct coverage.

Short answer

Align facts across the pages that will represent the new market, and avoid copy-spam mirrors. Thin mirrors are not market expansion, so the facts should match before extra market URLs are treated as coverage.

What to check

  • Align the facts first
  • Avoid copy-spam mirrors
  • Thin mirrors are not expansion
  • Product facts match first

What this does not claim

Preparing a new market this way does not guarantee rankings or demand in that market. Mika does not guarantee search rankings or AI answer citations because facts are aligned and copy-spam mirrors are avoided.

A practical example

Before adding a market, compare company name, product name, specifications, applications, claims, and question coverage so the new pages match the source facts. A translated copy that adds no distinct answer is a thin mirror, which is not market expansion.
