Definition

GEO Health asks whether people can find a clear, supportable answer from the site itself. GEO means Generative Engine Optimization, and this track looks at understanding, evidence, and answers.

Short answer

GEO Health is about a clear, supportable answer found on the site, using understanding, evidence, and answers. GEO means Generative Engine Optimization, and it is not geographic SEO.

What to check

  • Read the answer on the site itself
  • Look for evidence beside claims
  • Treat GEO as generative, not geographic
  • Keep GEO Health on its own scale

What this does not claim

A clear, supportable answer on the site does not guarantee that an AI product will repeat it. GEO Health does not guarantee inclusion, recommendation, or citation in any AI product.

A practical example

Suppose a visitor can read on the site who the company is, what the product does, and a spec placed next to the claim. GEO Health treats that as a clear supportable answer, and it does not treat GEO as geographic SEO.
