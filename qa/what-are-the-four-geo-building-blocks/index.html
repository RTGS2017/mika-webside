What are the four GEO building blocks?

GEO on the site is built from four blocks: entities, direct answers, evidence, and consistent context.

Definition

The four GEO building blocks are entities, direct answers, evidence, and consistent context. Together they describe what a page names, what it answers, what supports the answer, and whether that context stays consistent.

Short answer

The four building blocks are entities, direct answers, evidence, and consistent context. GEO work uses these four blocks, and using them is not a promise of inclusion in an AI answer.

What to check

  • Entities are named
  • Direct answers are present
  • Evidence supports the answer
  • Context stays consistent

What this does not claim

Using the four GEO building blocks does not guarantee inclusion, recommendation, or citation. Mika does not guarantee search rankings or AI answer citations, and GEO does not guarantee those outcomes.

A practical example

A product page can name the equipment as an entity, answer a suitability question directly, link specifications as evidence, and keep the same facts in English and Chinese. Those pieces are entities, direct answers, evidence, and consistent context.
