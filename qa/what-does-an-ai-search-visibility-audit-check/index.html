What does an AI search visibility audit check?

An AI search visibility audit reviews whether pages can be answered from named entities and supporting evidence.

Educational material only. Mika does not guarantee search rankings or AI answer citations.

Definition

An AI search visibility audit checks answerability, entities, and evidence. It reviews whether a page can be answered from the entities it names and the proof it shows, and it does not promise placement.

Short answer

The audit checks answerability, entities, and evidence. It does not promise placement in search results or in AI answers, so the output is a review of those three areas.

What to check

  • Answerability is checked
  • Entities are checked
  • Evidence is checked
  • Placement is not promised

What this does not claim

An AI search visibility audit does not guarantee placement in search or in AI answers. Mika does not guarantee search rankings or AI answer citations, and the audit does not promise placement.

A practical example

An audit can note that a product page names the equipment, answers a buyer question directly, and links specifications as evidence. That review covers answerability, entities, and evidence without promising where the page will appear.
