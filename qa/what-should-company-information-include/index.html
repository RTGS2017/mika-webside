What should company information include?

Company information should identify the business in plain language: who you are, what you sell, and where you operate.

Definition

Company information should say who you are, what you sell, and where you operate, without hype. Those three statements tell a reader which business the page describes, using facts rather than slogans.

Short answer

Say who you are, what you sell, and where you operate, without hype. Company information is an identification block, so unsupported praise does not substitute for those three facts.

What to check

  • Who the company is
  • What the company sells
  • Where the company operates
  • Written without any hype

What this does not claim

Clear company information does not guarantee that a search result or an AI answer will cite the page. Mika does not guarantee search rankings or AI answer citations because an about block names the business.

A practical example

A company block can say who operates the business, which products it sells, and the places where it operates. It stays on those facts and does not add hype in place of the missing identification.
