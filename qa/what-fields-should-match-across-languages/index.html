What fields should match across languages?

English and Chinese pages should agree on the same six fields before they are treated as one set of facts.

Educational material only. Mika does not guarantee search rankings or AI answer citations.

Definition

The fields that should match across languages are company name, product name, specifications, applications, claims, and question coverage. English at the root and Chinese under /zh/ should share these six facts rather than diverging into thin mirrors.

Short answer

Match company name, product name, specifications, applications, claims, and question coverage between languages. Those six fields are the language fields that should agree on bilingual pages.

What to check

  • Company name matches
  • Product name matches
  • Specifications match
  • Claims and coverage match

What this does not claim

Matching these fields does not guarantee equal rankings in each language. Mika does not guarantee search rankings or AI answer citations when company, product, specification, application, claim, and question-coverage fields agree.

A practical example

The English page and the /zh/ page should show the same company name, product name, specifications, applications, claims, and question coverage. Hreflang still marks en, zh-Hans, and x-default to English while those six fields stay aligned.
