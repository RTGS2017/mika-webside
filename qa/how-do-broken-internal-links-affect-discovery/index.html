How do broken internal links affect discovery?

Broken internal links waste crawl paths and trust. They do not by themselves prove a ranking.

Definition

Broken internal links waste discovery and trust because the path they offer does not lead to a working page. That waste is a crawl and trust problem, and it does not by itself prove a ranking.

Short answer

Broken internal links waste crawl paths and trust. A reader or crawler follows a link that fails, and that failure alone does not prove what a ranking will be.

What to check

  • Find internal links that fail
  • Count wasted crawl paths
  • Note the loss of reader trust
  • Do not treat the break as a ranking proof

What this does not claim

Broken internal links waste discovery and trust, and fixing them does not guarantee a position change. Mika does not guarantee that repairing those links proves or creates a ranking.

A practical example

Suppose a navigation link points to a product URL that returns an error status, so crawlers and readers follow a path that fails. That broken internal link wastes discovery and trust, and the failure alone does not prove what the ranking will be.
