Definition

SEO Health asks whether the live site is technically ready to be found and understood. It covers technical setup, structure, and content, and it stays on its own scale beside SEO Growth.

Short answer

SEO Health is the track for a live site that is technically ready to be found and understood. It reviews technical, structure, and content signals, and it is not blended into an overall score.

What to check

  • Check technical readiness
  • Check structure and content
  • Ask if the live site is understood
  • Keep this track off the growth scale

What this does not claim

A strong SEO Health reading does not guarantee a search position or a growth result. Technical, structure, and content readiness does not guarantee that any page will be chosen in search.

A practical example

Suppose headings, internal structure, and a crawlable URL make a product page possible to find and understand. SEO Health can describe that readiness, while SEO Growth separately asks which buyer intents are still open.
