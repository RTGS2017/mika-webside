What is hreflang for?

Hreflang is for naming language alternates, not for boosting a ranking.

Educational material only. Mika does not guarantee search rankings or AI answer citations.

On this page

  1. Definition
  2. Short answer
  3. What to check

  5. A practical example

Definition

Hreflang names language alternates so the relationship between versions is explicit. On this site those records are en, zh-Hans, and x-default to English, and they do not guess rankings.

Short answer

Hreflang is for naming language alternates such as en and zh-Hans. It is not a ranking boost, and on this site x-default points to English.

What to check

  • List en and zh-Hans alternates
  • Point x-default to English
  • Record language, not a rank boost
  • Do not guess positions from tags

What this does not claim

Hreflang does not guarantee any movement between language versions in search results. Mika does not guarantee that listing en, zh-Hans, and x-default will change positions.

A practical example

Suppose the English page is at the site root and the Chinese page is under /zh/. Hreflang can name en, zh-Hans, and x-default to English so the alternates are explicit, and that record does not guess which language will rank.
