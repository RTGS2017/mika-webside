What counts as evidence for a claim?

Evidence for a claim is a spec, a process, or verifiable context placed beside that claim.

Definition

Evidence is specs, process, or other verifiable context placed next to a claim. A slogan with no spec, process, or verifiable context does not count as evidence for that claim.

Short answer

What counts is a spec, a process, or other verifiable context sitting next to the claim. Slogans do not count, because a reader cannot verify them from the page.

What to check

  • Place a spec next to the claim
  • Or place a process next to the claim
  • Accept other verifiable context
  • Reject a slogan with no support

What this does not claim

Placing specs or process next to a claim does not guarantee that the claim will be cited or positioned in search. Mika does not guarantee that verifiable context produces a recommendation.

A practical example

Suppose a page claims a product suits a stated process and the process steps sit in the following paragraph. Those steps are evidence beside the claim, whereas a slogan with no spec or process is not evidence.
