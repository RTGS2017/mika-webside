What is search intent?

Search intent is the purpose of a query: informational, commercial, or transactional.

Definition

Search intent is the informational, commercial, or transactional purpose of a query. The label describes what the searcher is trying to do on the site, not a future position.

Short answer

Search intent is the purpose behind a query, and on this site that purpose is informational, commercial, or transactional. It classifies the query. It does not assign a rank.

What to check

  • Label informational purpose
  • Label commercial purpose
  • Label transactional purpose
  • Do not turn intent into a position

What this does not claim

Labeling intent as informational, commercial, or transactional does not guarantee the matching page will rank. Mika does not guarantee a result from intent mapping alone.

A practical example

Suppose one query asks how a process works, another asks which option fits a job, and a third asks how to request the product. Those are informational, commercial, and transactional intents, and the labels describe purpose rather than a promised position.
