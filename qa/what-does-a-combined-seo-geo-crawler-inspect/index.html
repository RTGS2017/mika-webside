What does a combined SEO and GEO crawler inspect?

A combined technical SEO and GEO crawler note keeps health, answerability, and accessibility apart, then explains each finding.

Educational material only. Mika does not guarantee search rankings or AI answer citations.

Definition

A combined technical SEO and GEO crawler note says to report health, answerability, and accessibility as separate columns. It also says to prioritize template-level defects, compare JavaScript and no-JavaScript rendering when rendering exists, and attach why, how, or if ignored to each finding.

Short answer

Inspect health, answerability, and accessibility as separate columns, and prioritize defects that repeat at the template level. When rendering exists, compare the JavaScript and no-JavaScript views, and record why a finding matters, how to address it, or if it was ignored.

What to check

  • Separate reporting columns
  • Template-level defects first
  • JavaScript and no-JavaScript
  • Why, how, or if ignored

What this does not claim

A combined SEO and GEO crawl does not guarantee better placement or that ignored items are resolved. Mika does not guarantee search rankings or AI answer citations, and separate columns are a reporting method rather than a promise.

A practical example

A missing canonical that repeats on every product URL can be prioritized as a template-level defect, with health, answerability, and accessibility kept in separate columns. If the site renders with JavaScript, the note compares that view with the no-JavaScript response and states why the defect matters, how to treat it, or if it was ignored.
