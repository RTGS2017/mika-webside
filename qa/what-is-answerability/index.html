What is answerability?

Answerability means the reader can take the answer from the page instead of inventing it.

Educational material only. Mika does not guarantee search rankings or AI answer citations.

Definition

Answerability means a page can state who you are, what you sell, and what is claimed without guesswork. A buyer question is answerable when the reader does not have to invent the meaning.

Short answer

Answerability is whether a page can answer a buyer question without the reader inventing the meaning. The page should say who you are, what you sell, and what is claimed.

What to check

  • State who the company is
  • State what the company sells
  • State the claim without guesswork
  • Stop the reader inventing meaning

What this does not claim

A page that states who you are and what you sell does not guarantee a search position or an AI citation. Mika does not guarantee a commercial result from answerability alone.

A practical example

Suppose a buyer lands on a page and can read who the company is, what it sells, and which claim is being made without inventing the meaning. That page has answerability for that question, because the answer is stated rather than guessed.
