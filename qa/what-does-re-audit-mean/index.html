What does re-audit mean?

A re-audit repeats the scan after the changes ship so you can see whether the finding changed.

Educational material only. Mika does not guarantee search rankings or AI answer citations.

Definition

Re-audit means running the scan again after changes ship, to see whether the finding changed. The comparison checks the finding itself and is not a way to predict rankings.

Short answer

Run the scan again after the changes ship and compare whether the finding changed. A re-audit reviews that change in the finding and does not predict rankings.

What to check

  • The scan runs again
  • The changes have shipped
  • The finding is compared
  • Rankings are not predicted

What this does not claim

A re-audit does not guarantee better rankings or that every finding will clear. Mika does not guarantee search rankings or AI answer citations, and the re-audit does not predict rankings.

A practical example

After a template canonical is corrected, a re-audit runs the scan again to see whether the indexation mismatch finding changed. The result reports the finding, and it does not predict a ranking.
