What to check

Say the name in the body

Put the concrete name in a sentence a stranger can read without opening a menu. The first screen should state the subject, the relationship, and the object in a sentence the reader can see. A heading that only says “solutions” does not identify the entity, and a model number buried in a download does not help a summary that never sees the sentence.

Answer the question in one passage

The question to answer in plain language is: whether a marked statement is also a sentence on the page? One short passage should be quotable on its own. If the passage still leaves a relationship that exists only in attributes, mark the coverage partial and add the missing constraint, range, or exclusion instead of another adjective.

Put evidence next to the claim

The evidence for this page is the HTML element that carries both the words and the RDFa attributes. Link it with anchor text that names the destination, and make sure the target URL returns the document you mean. A claim with no openable support is not citation-ready, even if the sentence sounds confident.

Repeat the same fact in both languages

English at the site root and Chinese under /zh/ should keep the same company name, product name, specification, and application. Translation may change word order. It should not invent a second rdfa or a second operating range. Hreflang marks the alternates. It does not guess which language an answer product will prefer.

A practical example

A relationship such as this product has an application of cooling water should appear as those words, not only as attributes on an empty span. If you would not show the triple to a buyer, do not hide it in attributes either. The contrast used across Mika’s teaching material is the same kind of contrast: a page of slogans versus a page that names the company, the product family, the application, and a constraint, with a datasheet beside the claim. That scene is illustrative. It is not a customer story, and it is not a measured result.
