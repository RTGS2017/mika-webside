Definition

An AI system is software that performs tasks by finding patterns in data. It does not have a private memory of your catalog. In Generative Engine Optimization this is a clarity problem, not a geographic-SEO trick and not a ranking lever. GEO keeps SEO Health, SEO Growth, GEO Health, and GEO Growth on separate tracks. How this shows up on a website

Answer systems do not receive a private briefing from sales. They read sentences, names, tables, and links that the HTML actually contains. When the text never settles what material about the company is available to read, the summary has to guess readable material that states the facts. That guess is a failure of the page, even when the site is technically reachable.

What to check

Say the name in the body

Put the concrete name in a sentence a stranger can read without opening a menu. The first screen should state the company description, the products, and the evidence on fetchable pages. A heading that only says “solutions” does not identify the entity, and a model number buried in a download does not help a summary that never sees the sentence.

Answer the question in one passage

The question to answer in plain language is: what material about the company is available to read? One short passage should be quotable on its own. If the passage still leaves readable material that states the facts, mark the coverage partial and add the missing constraint, range, or exclusion instead of another adjective.

Put evidence next to the claim

The evidence for this page is those pages, linked to each other. Link it with anchor text that names the destination, and make sure the target URL returns the document you mean. A claim with no openable support is not citation-ready, even if the sentence sounds confident.

Repeat the same fact in both languages

English at the site root and Chinese under /zh/ should keep the same company name, product name, specification, and application. Translation may change word order. It should not invent a second an ai system or a second operating range. Hreflang marks the alternates. It does not guess which language an answer product will prefer.

A practical example

An assistant that answers who you are will use whatever public text it can access. If that text is only a slogan, the answer will be a slogan. Publish the three-part identity instead. The contrast used across Mika’s teaching material is the same kind of contrast: a page of slogans versus a page that names the company, the product family, the application, and a constraint, with a datasheet beside the claim. That scene is illustrative. It is not a customer story, and it is not a measured result.
