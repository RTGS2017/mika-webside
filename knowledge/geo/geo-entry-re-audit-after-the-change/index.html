Definition

Shipping a document in a draft tool is not the same as shipping the URL. The re-audit fetches the canonical, reads the answer, checks the evidence link, and compares the other language. If the template overwrote the sentence, the finding stays open. SEO Health, SEO Growth, GEO Health, and GEO Growth are re-checked as separate columns, not as one celebratory number.

Short answer

A re-audit opens the live URL after the change and checks that the planned sentence, link, and language pair are actually in the response. The loop is scan, diagnose, blueprint, optimize, re-audit. The last step validates the page. It does not forecast a ranking.

What belongs on the page

Use the sample URL from the blueprint. Fetch it. Confirm the direct answer, the unit, and the datasheet link. Fetch the alternate language. Update the question state only if the HTML agrees. Write the next action if it does not.

A re-audit can be small. One URL and one question are enough when that was the blueprint. A full recrawl is a different job. Do not claim the entire site improved because one sentence shipped.

Re-audit is how the loop checks that the blueprint reached the live document. In Mika Visibility, GEO means generative engine optimization, not geographic targeting. The four building blocks are entities, direct answers, evidence, and consistent context. Citation readiness means a careful reader could quote a statement with less ambiguity. It is a quality of the page, not a promise that any answer product will cite it.

What to check

  • Fetch the live canonical, not the draft.
  • Check the sentence, the link, and the other language.
  • Keep the four tracks in separate notes.
  • Leave the finding open if the HTML does not match.

A practical example

The blueprint added a pressure unit to the CX-4 URL. The re-audit fetches that URL, sees the unit and the datasheet, and marks the question answered. The Chinese URL still lacks the unit, so that language stays partial. No lift is claimed.

What this does not claim

A passed re-audit does not guarantee rankings or AI citations. It only says the live page matches the planned fact.
