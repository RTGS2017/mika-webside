Name the four GEO dimensions without turning them into a score

Definition

The four tracks stay independent: SEO Health, SEO Growth, GEO Health, and GEO Growth. The dimensions above describe GEO Health qualities of pages you already have. GEO Growth asks where answer coverage can still improve. Mixing them into one number hides which check failed. The homepage sample scores are illustrative interface. Do not quote them as a result.

Short answer

GEO names the dimensions entity clarity, answerability, evidence coverage, consistency, and citation readiness. They are names for separate checks. Mika does not publish a blended overall score, and a high health finding does not become a growth score.

What belongs on the page

When you write a finding, name the dimension and the URL. Say what is missing in words: the product name, the answer, the evidence, the mismatch, or the ambiguity. Attach a next action. Do not attach a fake composite.

Five names are listed because citation readiness is often spoken with the other four. The building blocks remain entities, direct answers, evidence, and consistent context. Naming them is the practice. Scoring them on this page would contradict the product boundary.

The dimensions are a vocabulary. They are not a leaderboard. In Mika Visibility, GEO means generative engine optimization, not geographic targeting. The four building blocks are entities, direct answers, evidence, and consistent context. Citation readiness means a careful reader could quote a statement with less ambiguity. It is a quality of the page, not a promise that any answer product will cite it.

What to check

  • Use the dimension names.
  • Keep the four tracks separate.
  • Do not quote homepage sample scores as results.
  • Attach a URL and a next action instead of a blend.

A practical example

A report says “GEO 80.” The repair says the product URL is partial on answerability because the pressure unit is missing, and names the sentence to add. No replacement score is offered.

What this does not claim

Naming a dimension does not guarantee rankings or AI citations, and it is not an overall score.
