Short answer

A direct answer block is a short passage, high on the page, that states the subject, the constraint, and the fact in sentences a stranger can quote. It is a GEO Health building block. It does not promise that any answer product will quote it.

What belongs on the page

Choose one primary question for the URL. Write two or three sentences. Name the product the way the rest of the site names it. Include the limit in the same passage. Link the evidence immediately after, with an anchor that names the document.

The block can be short. If it still omits the exclusion, coverage is partial. Add the exclusion. Do not add another adjective. The same fact should appear in English at the root and in Chinese under /zh/.

Mika calls this a direct answer. It is not a guaranteed snippet. In Mika Visibility, GEO means generative engine optimization, not geographic targeting. The four building blocks are entities, direct answers, evidence, and consistent context. Citation readiness means a careful reader could quote a statement with less ambiguity. It is a quality of the page, not a promise that any answer product will cite it.

What to check

  • One primary question for the URL.
  • The first passage names subject, constraint, and fact.
  • The passage is HTML text.
  • The same fact appears in both languages.

A practical example

The teaching question is which product suits low-rise concrete placement. The block names the family, the rise constraint, and the datasheet. A page that only says “engineered for builders” has no block yet. The scene is illustrative, not a customer.

What this does not claim

A direct answer block does not guarantee search rankings or inclusion in ChatGPT, Gemini, or AI Overviews.
