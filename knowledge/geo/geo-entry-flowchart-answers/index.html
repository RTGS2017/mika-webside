Definition

A selection flowchart is a sequence of questions that ends in a named product or an exclusion. In Generative Engine Optimization this is a clarity problem, not a geographic-SEO trick and not a ranking lever. GEO keeps SEO Health, SEO Growth, GEO Health, and GEO Growth on separate tracks. A page can be easy to crawl and still leave the branch written as text unresolved.

How this shows up on a website

Answer systems do not receive a private briefing from sales. They read sentences, names, tables, and links that the HTML actually contains. When the text never settles which branch a buyer should follow for this duty, the summary has to guess the branch written as text. That guess is a failure of the page, even when the site is technically reachable.

A selection flowchart is a sequence of questions that ends in a named product or an exclusion. The practical move is small and specific: the questions, the branches, and the destination product as text. Put that passage where a buyer lands, not only in a slogan line or a title attribute. Then connect it to the specification of the product at the end of the branch, so the claim and the support are on speaking terms.

Flowcharts are useful because they are direct answers with constraints. They become decoration when the only copy is an image. Repeat the decision in HTML. Link each leaf to the product page that carries the evidence. Do not draw a branch you cannot support.

Question coverage still has three honest states: answered, partial, and uncovered. A page that names the topic but never answers which branch a buyer should follow for this duty is partial at best. A site that never asks the question at all is uncovered. GEO Growth is the place to record that gap. GEO Health is the place to record whether the pages you already have can support an answer. Neither scale is an overall blended score.

What to check

Say the name in the body

Put the concrete name in a sentence a stranger can read without opening a menu. The first screen should state the questions, the branches, and the destination product as text. A heading that only says “solutions” does not identify the entity, and a model number buried in a download does not help a summary that never sees the sentence.

Answer the question in one passage

The question to answer in plain language is: which branch a buyer should follow for this duty? One short passage should be quotable on its own. If the passage still leaves the branch written as text, mark the coverage partial and add the missing constraint, range, or exclusion instead of another adjective.

Put evidence next to the claim

The evidence for this page is the specification of the product at the end of the branch. Link it with anchor text that names the destination, and make sure the target URL returns the document you mean. A claim with no openable support is not citation-ready, even if the sentence sounds confident.

Repeat the same fact in both languages

English at the site root and Chinese under /zh/ should keep the same company name, product name, specification, and application. Translation may change word order. It should not invent a second a selection flowchart or a second operating range. Hreflang marks the alternates. It does not guess which language an answer product will prefer.

A practical example

A PNG of a flowchart with no text leaves the branch unreadable. Write: if the fluid is water and the pressure is within the stated limit, use NV-40; otherwise this family does not apply. The contrast used across Mika’s teaching material is the same kind of contrast: a page of slogans versus a page that names the company, the product family, the application, and a constraint, with a datasheet beside the claim. That scene is illustrative. It is not a customer story, and it is not a measured result.
