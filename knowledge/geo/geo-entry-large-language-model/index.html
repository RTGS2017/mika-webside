How this shows up on a website

Answer systems do not receive a private briefing from sales. They read sentences, names, tables, and links that the HTML actually contains. When the text never settles which product facts are actually written down, the summary has to guess the specification the model was expected to know. That guess is a failure of the page, even when the site is technically reachable.

The practical move is small and specific: the specification in sentences on the product URL. Put that passage where a buyer lands, not only in a slogan line or a title attribute. Treat model output as a draft someone else might produce, not as a source of truth about your product. If a draft invents a rating, the remedy is a page that states the rating you do support, or a page that states you do not publish one. Do not paste the draft onto the site as if it were a measurement.

Question coverage still has three honest states: answered, partial, and uncovered. A page that names the topic but never answers which product facts are actually written down is partial at best. A site that never asks the question at all is uncovered. GEO Growth is the place to record that gap. GEO Health is the place to record whether the pages you already have can support an answer. Neither scale is an overall blended score.

What to check

Say the name in the body

Put the concrete name in a sentence a stranger can read without opening a menu. The first screen should state the specification in sentences on the product URL. A heading that only says “solutions” does not identify the entity, and a model number buried in a download does not help a summary that never sees the sentence.

Answer the question in one passage

The question to answer in plain language is: which product facts are actually written down? One short passage should be quotable on its own. If the passage still leaves the specification the model was expected to know, mark the coverage partial and add the missing constraint, range, or exclusion instead of another adjective.

Put evidence next to the claim

The evidence for this page is the datasheet linked beside those sentences. Link it with anchor text that names the destination, and make sure the target URL returns the document you mean. A claim with no openable support is not citation-ready, even if the sentence sounds confident.

Repeat the same fact in both languages

English at the site root and Chinese under /zh/ should keep the same company name, product name, specification, and application. Translation may change word order. It should not invent a second a large language model or a second operating range. Hreflang marks the alternates. It does not guess which language an answer product will prefer.

A practical example

Asking a model which seal fits a solvent does not create the compatibility table. Publish the table, or say the table is not published and the question is uncovered. The contrast used across Mika’s teaching material is the same kind of contrast: a page of slogans versus a page that names the company, the product family, the application, and a constraint, with a datasheet beside the claim. That scene is illustrative. It is not a customer story, and it is not a measured result.
