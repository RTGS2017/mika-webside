How this shows up on a website

Answer systems do not receive a private briefing from sales. They read sentences, names, tables, and links that the HTML actually contains. When the text never settles which section holds the supporting fact, the summary has to guess the name of the section that holds the fact. That guess is a failure of the page, even when the site is technically reachable.

A cross-reference is a pointer from one passage to a named section or page. The practical move is small and specific: the section title or anchor, specific enough to open the right block. Put that passage where a buyer lands, not only in a slogan line or a title attribute. Then connect it to the target section, present on a URL that works, so the claim and the support are on speaking terms.

Cross-references are how evidence stays next to a claim even when the full table lives elsewhere. They fail when the section was renamed and the pointer was not. During a re-audit, open the sample URL and follow the pointer. A next action is to repair the pointer or move the fact.

Question coverage still has three honest states: answered, partial, and uncovered. A page that names the topic but never answers which section holds the supporting fact is partial at best. A site that never asks the question at all is uncovered. GEO Growth is the place to record that gap. GEO Health is the place to record whether the pages you already have can support an answer. Neither scale is an overall blended score.

What to check

Say the name in the body

Put the concrete name in a sentence a stranger can read without opening a menu. The first screen should state the section title or anchor, specific enough to open the right block. A heading that only says “solutions” does not identify the entity, and a model number buried in a download does not help a summary that never sees the sentence.

Answer the question in one passage

The question to answer in plain language is: which section holds the supporting fact? One short passage should be quotable on its own. If the passage still leaves the name of the section that holds the fact, mark the coverage partial and add the missing constraint, range, or exclusion instead of another adjective.

Put evidence next to the claim

The evidence for this page is the target section, present on a URL that works. Link it with anchor text that names the destination, and make sure the target URL returns the document you mean. A claim with no openable support is not citation-ready, even if the sentence sounds confident.

Repeat the same fact in both languages

English at the site root and Chinese under /zh/ should keep the same company name, product name, specification, and application. Translation may change word order. It should not invent a second a cross-reference or a second operating range. Hreflang marks the alternates. It does not guess which language an answer product will prefer.
