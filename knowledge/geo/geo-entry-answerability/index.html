Definition

A page can be crawlable and still not answerable. SEO Health can pass while GEO Health records that the question is partial. The missing piece is often the constraint, the unit, or the exclusion. Uncovered means the site never asks the question. Partial means it gestures at the topic. Answered means the passage can stand alone.

Short answer

Answerability means a careful reader can answer the page’s primary question from the text, the table, and the links on that URL. It is a named GEO dimension without a score here. It does not mean an AI product will answer with you.

What belongs on the page

Read the URL as if you did not have the sales call. Try to answer the primary question in one breath. If you cannot, the page is not answerable yet. Add the missing fact. Do not add a second page that repeats the gap.

Answerability is not word count. A short specification can be answerable. A long essay that never commits to a range is not. The dimension sits beside entity clarity, evidence coverage, consistency, and citation readiness. Names only. No scores on this page.

Answerability is a named dimension. This entry does not score it. In Mika Visibility, GEO means generative engine optimization, not geographic targeting. The four building blocks are entities, direct answers, evidence, and consistent context. Citation readiness means a careful reader could quote a statement with less ambiguity. It is a quality of the page, not a promise that any answer product will cite it.

What to check

  • Name the primary question.
  • See if the text alone answers it.
  • Add the missing constraint instead of another URL.
  • Record answered, partial, or uncovered.

A practical example

The question is which rotor is safe above a stated speed. The page praises reliability and omits the rotor limit. It is not answerable. The repair adds the limit and the exclusion. No score is attached.
