Short answer

Entity clarity means a reader can tell which company and which product a sentence describes, because the stable names are in the text. It is a named GEO dimension. It is not a score on this page, and it is not a knowledge-panel promise.

What belongs on the page

In the first screen, write the company name, the product name, and what it is. Use that spelling on the About page and in both languages. List aliases under the preferred name instead of switching names by page.

Clarity can be one sentence. A long history that never names the product you sell is not clear. The dimension is named here without a number. Homepage sample scores are illustrative interface, not this product’s result.

Entity clarity is a named dimension. This page does not assign it a score. In Mika Visibility, GEO means generative engine optimization, not geographic targeting. The four building blocks are entities, direct answers, evidence, and consistent context. Citation readiness means a careful reader could quote a statement with less ambiguity. It is a quality of the page, not a promise that any answer product will cite it.

What to check

  • Put the company and product names in HTML text.
  • Keep one spelling across key pages and languages.
  • Treat model numbers as identifiers.
  • Do not publish a numeric entity score as a result.

A practical example

The hero says “platform.” The datasheet says “controller CX-4.” The repair’s first sentence is that the company sells the CX-4 controller for a named application. No panel placement is promised.

What this does not claim

Clearer entities do not guarantee rankings, knowledge panels, or AI citations.
