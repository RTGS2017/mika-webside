Citation readiness is a quality of the page, not a placement

Citation readiness means a careful reader could quote a sentence from the page with less ambiguity about the subject, the fact, and the source of the fact. It describes the page. It does not promise a citation in any AI product.

Definition

GEO names citation readiness as a dimension, not as a delivered mention. The sentence is ready when it names the entity, states the fact, and points at evidence. A slogan is hard to quote because it could belong to anyone. Primary material on your site is the page you publish. Do not fabricate a journal citation to look ready.

Short answer

Citation readiness means a careful reader could quote a sentence from the page with less ambiguity about the subject, the fact, and the source of the fact. It describes the page. It does not promise a citation in any AI product.

What belongs on the page

Take the sentence you hope someone could repeat. Remove it from the page and see if it still names the product and the limit. If it does not, rewrite it until it does. Keep that wording in both languages.

Readiness is not a bibliography of famous papers. One datasheet link can be enough. A wall of logos is not a citation. The existing citation entry on this site is about attribution. This entry is about the quality of your own sentence.

Citation readiness is a named dimension without a score and without a placement promise. In Mika Visibility, GEO means generative engine optimization, not geographic targeting. The four building blocks are entities, direct answers, evidence, and consistent context. Citation readiness means a careful reader could quote a statement with less ambiguity. It is a quality of the page, not a promise that any answer product will cite it.

What to check

  • The sentence names the product when read alone.
  • It states the fact and the limit.
  • It points at evidence you publish.
  • It does not rely on a fake citation.

A practical example

“Industry leading” cannot be quoted as a fact. “The HX-2 exchanger is rated to the stated pressure in datasheet HX-2-D” can be checked. The repair replaces the first with the second. No mention count is claimed.

What this does not claim

A quotable sentence does not guarantee rankings or inclusion in ChatGPT, Gemini, AI Overviews, or any other product.
