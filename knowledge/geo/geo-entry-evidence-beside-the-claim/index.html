Put the evidence beside the claim it supports

Evidence beside a claim means the sentence and the support are on speaking terms: a datasheet, a standard, or a named section on the same site, linked so a reader can open it. GEO measures whether the claim is supportable. It does not guarantee a citation.

Definition

A claim with no openable support is not citation-ready, even if it sounds precise. The evidence should use a stable URL that returns the document you mean. Anchor text should name that document, not “click here.” If the evidence file 404s, the proof is gone. Do not invent test results to fill the gap.

Short answer

Evidence beside a claim means the sentence and the support are on speaking terms: a datasheet, a standard, or a named section on the same site, linked so a reader can open it. GEO measures whether the claim is supportable. It does not guarantee a citation.

What belongs on the page

Write the claim, then the document name, then the link. Keep the number and the unit in the sentence so the link is not the only place they exist. Repeat that pair in each language.

One relevant document is better than a pile of unrelated badges. Customer logos and award seals you cannot explain are not evidence. The homepage entity path is company, products, applications, technical information, documentation, evidence.

Evidence is a building block of GEO Health, separate from whether anyone later quotes you. In Mika Visibility, GEO means generative engine optimization, not geographic targeting. The four building blocks are entities, direct answers, evidence, and consistent context. Citation readiness means a careful reader could quote a statement with less ambiguity. It is a quality of the page, not a promise that any answer product will cite it.

What to check

  • Name the support in the same passage as the claim.
  • Use a link that returns the document.
  • Keep the number in HTML, not only in the file.
  • Refuse invented results and unexplained logos.

A practical example

A page says a coating “resists chemicals.” The repair says which family, which exposure the datasheet covers, and links that datasheet. It does not add a lab ranking. The contrast with a slogan page is illustrative.

What this does not claim

Evidence beside a claim does not guarantee rankings or AI citations. Citation readiness is not placement.
