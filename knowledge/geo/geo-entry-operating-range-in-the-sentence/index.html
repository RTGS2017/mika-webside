Put the operating range in the same sentence as the number

An operating range is answerable when the number, the unit, and the condition are in the same sentence as the product name. A number in a caption, with the unit somewhere else, is partial. GEO wants that sentence on the product URL.

Definition

Ranges are how a buyer excludes the wrong product. “Up to” without a unit, or a unit without the condition, invites a wrong reading. Say the unit and the range together. If there is a minimum and a maximum, say both. Translation must not drop one end. This is the same discipline as evidence: the fact should be checkable without a meeting.

Short answer

An operating range is answerable when the number, the unit, and the condition are in the same sentence as the product name. A number in a caption, with the unit somewhere else, is partial. GEO wants that sentence on the product URL.

What belongs on the page

Draft the sentence before the hero slogan. Include the product name, the number, the unit, and the condition or exclusion. Repeat it in the other language. Link the datasheet that contains the full table, without removing the sentence.

A table can extend the sentence. It should not be the only place the range exists if the table is an image. HTML text remains the answer. Do not invent a tighter tolerance than the document states.

The operating range is a fact pattern GEO expects next to the entity. In Mika Visibility, GEO means generative engine optimization, not geographic targeting. The four building blocks are entities, direct answers, evidence, and consistent context. Citation readiness means a careful reader could quote a statement with less ambiguity. It is a quality of the page, not a promise that any answer product will cite it.

What to check

  • Put number, unit, and condition in one sentence.
  • Name the product in that sentence.
  • Keep both ends of the range in every language.
  • Do not leave the only copy inside an image.

A practical example

The page says “rated 16.” It does not say 16 what, or under which condition. The repair says the valve is rated to 16 bar at the stated temperature, per the named datasheet. No performance award is added.
