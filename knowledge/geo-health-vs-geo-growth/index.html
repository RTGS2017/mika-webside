Direct answer

GEO Health asks whether people can find a clear, supportable answer about your business from the site itself: stable entities, direct answers, and evidence tied to claims. GEO Growth asks where answer coverage, citation readiness, and expansion can still improve — uncovered queries and thin answer surfaces.

GEO Health and GEO Growth are independent of each other and of SEO Health and SEO Growth. There is no combined overall score. Health explains what is usable now. Growth explains what is still missing. Strong clarity today does not erase tomorrow’s open questions.

Health vs Growth comparison

Modern search often shows short answers, summaries, and source links alongside classic results. GEO work focuses on whether your website can support that kind of understanding — without promising that any system will cite or recommend you.

  • GEO Health checks understanding, evidence, and answerability on pages that already exist: who you are, what you sell, where you operate, and whether claims can be checked.
  • GEO Growth checks query gaps, answer gaps, citation readiness, and expansion opportunities — the questions buyers ask that your site still leaves partial or blank.

Keep the four-capability frame in view. SEO Health and SEO Growth handle crawl, structure, and search coverage. GEO Health and GEO Growth handle answer clarity and unanswered demand. Mixing them into one KPI hides whether you need foundation fixes, coverage work, clearer entities, or new answer blocks.

Scan results should still follow discovery → diagnosis → blueprint → re-audit. Diagnosis separates “hard to extract or unsupported today” from “not covered yet.” The blueprint can then assign page-level actions — definitions, FAQ blocks, specs, proof — with a re-audit check after publish.

Example

A machinery brand has consistent company names, clear product titles, and a specs table on each product page. GEO Health looks reasonable: entities are recognizable and claims point to measurable attributes.

GEO Growth can still be open. Buyers ask how to choose between two duty classes, what installation constraints apply indoors, or which certificate to request for a tender. If those answers live only in sales chats — or are buried in a PDF without a short page-level answer — the growth side still shows gaps.

Improving growth here means adding direct answer blocks and evidence links on the pages that should own those questions. It does not mean inventing a higher “GEO score” by averaging health and opportunity. And it never means guaranteeing that an AI answer surface will quote the new copy.

FAQ

What does GEO Health actually check on my website?

GEO Health asks whether company and product entities are clear, whether key buyer questions have direct answers on real pages, and whether claims point to supportable evidence. It measures understandability and reference readiness on the live site — not whether any AI product will cite you.

If GEO Health looks strong, why would GEO Growth still matter?

Health describes what is already clear and supportable. Growth describes questions, answer surfaces, and expansion opportunities that are still open. A site can explain who it is and still leave commercial or application questions uncovered. High health does not automatically mean high growth room is closed.

Is GEO a replacement for SEO Health and SEO Growth?

No. SEO and GEO stay on separate tracks. SEO Health and SEO Growth cover crawl, structure, and search coverage. GEO Health and GEO Growth cover answer clarity, evidence, and unanswered questions. Mika never blends the four into one overall score.
