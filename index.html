Product logic

There are two different visibility problems.

Search ranks pages. AI answers questions. Treating them as one score hides the work. Mika measures both on the same site — without mixing them into a single KPI.

SEO

Search Visibility

Whether crawlers can fetch, index, and understand the site: crawl paths, indexability, architecture, on-page fundamentals, internal links, multilingual hreflang, structured data, images, and mobile.

Crawl & index Architecture On-page Internal links hreflang JSON-LD

GEO

AI Visibility

Whether AI systems can retrieve a clear entity, extract a direct answer, find supporting evidence, and cite the page. GEO is retrieval and citation readiness — not a forecast of ChatGPT or AI Overviews rank.

Entity clarity Answerability Evidence Citation Answer surface Cross-language

Workflow

From audit to continuous growth.

Optimization is not finished when tasks are written. It is finished when a re-audit confirms the facts changed.

01

Audit

Scan the live source or dist. Health and Growth stay split. Coverage and confidence travel with the report.

02

Diagnose

Issue groups by rule. Sample URLs. Severity. No blended “site score” as the story.

03

Blueprint

P0–P4 guidance. Page, type, changes, do-not list. execute: false until a human or a later optimizer is invoked.

04

Act

Fix crawl first, then entity and content. Do not fabricate certificates, customers, prices, or rankings.

05

Re-audit

The last step is not Done. Rescan the same paths. Compare Health, Growth, and the issue list.
